# Subscription Review

Read recurring charges and a matching SaaS admin usage export. Confirm merchant, workspace/account and observation window. Zero activity is a review candidate, not proof cancellation is safe. Ask the owner about archival access, integrations, annual contracts and upcoming work. Calculate avoidable monthly/annual spend with renewal timing and replacement costs. Suggest plan consolidation, seat reduction, existing overlapping tools or a vendor quote; do not invent current competitor prices. Draft cancellation emails for review, with export/retention and confirmation language. Never send messages, delete accounts or cancel without a separate explicit request.
